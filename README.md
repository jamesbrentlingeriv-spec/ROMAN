# A Roman Witness — Official Book Website
**Author:** James Brentlinger  
**First Edition:** 2026 (Professionally typeset on Reedsy)

An immersive, fully functional companion website for the epic historical novel **"A Roman Witness"**, chronicling the story of Marcus Aurelius Severus—a nineteen-year-old Roman patrician auditor sent to Jerusalem during the fateful Passover of 33 A.D. who abandons imperial Rome to follow Jesus Christ.

---

## 🏛️ Website Features

1. **Interactive Cartographic Map of Ancient Jerusalem (Anno Domini XXXIII)**:
   - High-resolution vintage parchment cartography depicting 1st-century Jerusalem.
   - Interactive glowing markers with pulse animations for all major settings in the book:
     - **Golgotha (The Skull / Locus Calvariae)**
     - **Pontius Pilate's House / Praetorium (Lithostrotos / The Stone Pavement & Bema)**
     - **The Second Temple & Royal Stoa / Solomon's Porch**
     - **Antonia Fortress & Tabularium Archives**
     - **Garden of Gethsemane & Mount of Olives**
     - **Tomb of Joseph of Arimathea**
     - **The Via Dolorosa & Gennath Gate**
     - **The Upper Room on Mount Zion**
     - **House of Simon the Tanner**
     - **Pool of Siloam & Hezekiah's Subterranean Aqueduct**
     - **The Well at Bethany**
     - **The City of David & Ophel Ridge**
   - Click any location to open a synchronized side dossier featuring high-resolution historical scene artwork, historical context, key figures, and verbatim book excerpts.
   - Filter landmarks by: *All Locations*, *Passion & Crucifixion*, *Roman & Temple Power*, *Apostolic Underground*.
   - Smooth mouse/touch pan, zoom controls (+, -, reset), and quick-jump buttons.

2. **Character Gallery & Detailed Dossiers**:
   - High-fidelity portrait artwork for:
     - **Marcus Aurelius Severus** (Patrician commissioner with the boar signet ring)
     - **Jesus the Nazarene** (The carpenter & King of Kings in seamless Galilean tunic)
     - **Titus** (Weathered legionary veteran freedman)
     - **Pontius Pilate** (Imperial Prefect washing hands in silver basin)
     - **Simon Peter** (Apostle breaking barley bread)
     - **Mary of Nazareth** (Mother of Jesus in dark blue veil)
     - **John, Son of Zebedee** (Beloved disciple with reed pen)
     - **Joseph Caiaphas** (High Priest in Babylonian silk)
     - **Tribune Valerius** (Pilate's ruthless staff officer in brass armor)
   - Filter characters by faction (*Roman Authority*, *The Disciples & The Way*, *The Temple Leadership*, *The Holy Women*).
   - Interactive dossier modal with character profiles, novel arcs, signature symbols, and quotes.

3. **Complete 20-Chapter Reader & Study Guide**:
   - Comprehensive breakdown of all 20 chapters from the original novel.
   - Full chapter summaries, historical themes, and verbatim reading passages from the book.
   - Reader customization:
     - Theme toggle: **🌙 Night Mode**, **📜 Papyrus Parchment**, **🍂 Sepia Classical**.
     - Dynamic font sizing (A- / A+).
     - Previous / Next navigation controls.

4. **Chronological Holy Week Timeline**:
   - Day-by-day interactive journey tracking Marcus's path from his arrival at Caesarea Maritima, through Passion Week in Jerusalem, to the mission in Antioch.

5. **Web Audio Ambient Soundscape**:
   - Synthesizes atmospheric desert winds, resonant ancient temple bell chimes, and gentle Greek/Roman lyre melodies using the Web Audio API (zero audio file dependencies).
   - Mute/unmute toggle and volume control slider.

6. **Roman Law & Sacred Artifacts Lore**:
   - Explanations of key historical Roman legal doctrines (*Lex Julia de Maiestate*, *Lex Sempronia*, *Patria Potestas*, *Exheredatus*, *Korban*).
   - Sacred relic cards (The Severan Boar Ring, The Soudarion Face-Cloth, The Broken Barley Loaf).

## 🚀 Running the Website

The website is fully built and ready to run locally:

```bash
# Start the built-in HTTP server
node server.js
# or
npm start
```

Then open your browser to:
`http://localhost:3000`
